About Driftlight

A quiet game about sailing a little skiff over a sea of clouds. There is no timer and nothing can hurt you. Explore, gather drift-seeds, read the old keeper's stones, and wake the three dark lighthouses of the Amberreach.

Controls

Arrow keys or WASD to sail, or tap or click where you'd like to go (hold to steer). Press E, Space, or tap the glowing prompt to interact. M opens the map, J the journal. Your voyage is saved on this device.

Privacy

Privacy. Driftlight uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which features get used, so the app can be improved. You don't need an account, and nothing you type into the app is collected. Your data will never be sold or given away.
